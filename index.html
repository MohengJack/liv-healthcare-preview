Meet the founder

Steven Gu

Founder & Accredited Exercise Physiologist

Steven founded LIV Healthcare to make high-quality allied health care accessible — helping people improve their health, restore function and get back to the activities that matter to them.

He specialises in healthy ageing, functional rehabilitation and work-related injury recovery. Through individualised exercise therapy, functional assessments, soft tissue techniques and education, he builds tailored, evidence-based programs around each person's needs and goals.

  • Bachelor of Exercise PhysiologyUniversity of New South Wales
  • Accredited Exercise PhysiologistExercise & Sports Science Australia (ESSA)
  • SIRA-accredited providerState Insurance Regulatory Authority, NSW
  • Additional certificationsDry needling · Kinesiology taping · Soft tissue mobilisation
